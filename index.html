Platform terms & pricing

  • Finance: flat 8% service fee on the approved amount, disclosed before you accept. Late repayment attracts a 2% weekly penalty on the outstanding balance.
  • Pay: bill settlement is free for wallets in good standing. A ₦50 processing fee applies to float-backed settlements for overdue wallets.
  • Co: 0% interest on essentials. Orders split into up to 4 installments; missed installments pause new orders until caught up.
  • Floats: customer balances are held in ring-fenced floats per arm, reviewed daily, and never lent out.
  • Privacy: demo build — data lives in your browser's localStorage only.
